Library Help Center

Welcome to Smart Library. Here is everything you need to know about using the library efficiently.

🔍

1. Finding & Searching Books

Use the search bar on the homepage or catalogue to find books by title, author, category, or ISBN. You can preview covers, summaries, and real-time stock availability.

📖

2. Borrowing Physical Copies

Logged-in students can borrow up to 3 books simultaneously for 14 days. Submit a borrow request and pick up the book once approved by library staff.

💻

3. Reading Digital PDFs

Books marked with PDF icons can be read directly inside your browser using our built-in reader without requiring physical checkout.

💳

4. Managing Overdue & Fines

Avoid overdue fines by returning books on time or requesting an extension before the due date. Outstanding fines can be paid securely via Chapa (Telebirr/CBE) or Stripe.

Terms of Service

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering for or using Smart Library, you agree to comply with all library rules, guidelines, and policies described herein.

2. Account Responsibility

Users must provide accurate registration details and maintain the security of their login credentials. You are responsible for all borrow transactions conducted under your library account.

3. Book Care & Return Policy

All borrowed books remain the property of the library. Borrowers are expected to keep items in good physical condition and return them on or before the due date. Lost or damaged books will incur a replacement fee.

4. Late Returns & Fines

Failure to return items on time will result in late fines. Borrowing privileges may be temporarily suspended if outstanding fines exceed the allowable balance.

5. Digital Content & Copyright

Electronic books and PDFs provided through Smart Library are strictly for educational and individual non-commercial use. Redistribution or unauthorized modification is prohibited.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the personal information required to administer library accounts and borrowing activities, including your name, email address, optional contact number, and borrowing history.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used solely to manage your library membership, authenticate logins, send critical notification emails (loan confirmations, due soon reminders, overdue notices), and process fine receipts.

3. Data Protection & Security

All passwords are encrypted using secure PBKDF2 hashing. Sessions and CSRF tokens are protected by secure HTTP-only cookies and encrypted HTTPS transport. We never sell or share user data with third-party advertisers.

4. Your Rights

You may view and update your profile details anytime in your Account Settings. For inquiries or account deletion requests, contact us at mubarekali974@gmail.com.
